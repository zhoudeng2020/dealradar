LocalEatRadar Privacy Policy

Effective 10 September 2026

LocalEatRadar helps you find nearby food and drink deals that are on right now. This policy explains exactly what the app does with your data. The short version: the app has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics, and it does not build a profile of you.

What the app collects

DataWhyWhere it goes
Your approximate or precise location To find deals near you, sort them by distance, and estimate how long it takes to reach each venue Held in memory on your device while the app is open. Your coordinates are sent to Google and Open‑Meteo as described below.
Your filter choices (deal type, travel mode, time, city) To show the feed you asked for Stays on your device. Not transmitted.

The app asks for location only while you are using it. It does not request background location and cannot see where you are when it is closed. If you decline the permission, the app still works — it falls back to the centre of the city you select, and nothing about your position leaves the device.

What the app does not collect

There is no sign-up and no user account. The app does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, calendar, or health data. It contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, and no third-party tracking of any kind. Nothing you do in the app is linked to your identity, and no data is sold or shared for advertising or marketing.

Third-party services

To work, the app sends requests to the following services. Each receives only what it needs for that request.

ServiceWhat it receivesPurpose
Google Places API Venue names and identifiers. Not your location. Current opening hours for each venue
Google Routes API Your coordinates as the starting point, plus venue coordinates Travel time to each venue by walking, transit, or driving
Open‑Meteo Your coordinates, rounded to the local area Current temperature and rain forecast, used to adjust deal ranking
Expo (EAS Update) App version and device platform Checking for app updates

These providers handle the data they receive under their own privacy policies: Google, Open‑Meteo, and Expo. LocalEatRadar has no control over their practices and does not receive any data back about you from them.

Retention

The app keeps no server-side records, because it has no server of its own. Your location exists only in the app's memory while it is running and is discarded when the app closes. Deal and venue information ships inside the app itself.

Accuracy of deal information

Deals are compiled from venue websites and public listings and can change or expire without notice. The app shows a confidence indicator on each deal. Please confirm with the venue before relying on an offer.

Children

LocalEatRadar is not directed at children. Because it lists happy-hour and other alcohol-related offers, it is rated for users aged 17 and over.

Your choices

You can revoke location access at any time in your device settings — on iOS under Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services, and on Android under Settings › Apps › EatRadar › Permissions. The app continues to work without it. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and the revised version will be posted at this address. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
